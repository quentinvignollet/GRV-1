Overview

Great Circle & Rhumb Line Visualizer is an interactive teaching tool for student pilots and flight instructors preparing for the EASA and UK CAA ATPL / CPL theoretical knowledge examinations (General Navigation).

Great circle vs rhumb line

Place two points anywhere on a 3D globe and compare the great circle and the rhumb line joining them: distances, initial and final true tracks, vertex, convergency and conversion angle, with a flight simulation that shows the true track changing along the great circle and staying constant along the rhumb line.

Views and charts

Latitude and longitude are constructed on the globe and on top, bottom, profile and meridian-section views. The same routes are drawn on Mercator, Lambert conformal conic (adjustable parallel of origin) and polar stereographic charts, where chart convergency is compared with earth convergency.

Slice along GC cuts the globe through its centre along the great-circle plane. The half nearer to you stays in place with all its construction; the other half is pulled away, its cut face outlined by the great circle.
